The Earth

Look, Earth is this magnificent blue marble hurtling through space at 67,000 miles per hour, which is great, truly great, except we're all clinging to it like passengers on a cosmic bus with no seatbelts. It's the only known planet to support life, which sounds impressive until you realize that the life it supports includes both David Attenborough and people who argue with flight attendants. Earth has incredible features: majestic oceans that we've filled with plastic bags, lush rainforests we're cutting down at record speed, and an atmosphere that's perfect for breathing that we're actively making worse! The planet literally provides everything we need, and our response has been to treat it like a hotel room we're not getting the deposit back on. That's Earth! Beautiful, fragile, and currently being managed with all the foresight of a teenager with their first credit card.
