The Moon

The Moon is Earth’s constant companion, silently tracing its path through space. From its pale surface, marked by ancient impacts, it reflects the Sun’s light back to our world. It has watched over Earth for billions of years, steadying its rotation and shaping the rhythm of the tides. With no atmosphere to soften its scars, the Moon preserves a record of the solar system’s early history. Its gravity subtly influences life below, from ocean movements to natural cycles. Though lifeless, it has guided human curiosity and exploration. The Moon remains a quiet witness to change, unwavering and familiar. It is a reminder of how closely connected our world is to the cosmos.
