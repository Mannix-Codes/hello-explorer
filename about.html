The Moon

The Moon: Earth's natural satellite and permanent celestial stalker that's been orbiting us for 4.5 billion years like an ex who "just wants to stay friends." It's this gray, cratered ball of rock that has absolutely nothing going on. No atmosphere, no water, no life, just regolith and the footprints of twelve Americans who visited in the '60s and apparently decided, "yeah, we're good, we don't need to come back." The Moon literally controls our ocean tides, and yet we barely acknowledge it unless we're writing a love song. It's been getting pummeled by asteroids for billions of years, taking hits to make Earth safer, basically the planetary equivalent of a loyal friend who throws themselves in front of a bus for you. And we've repaid it by planting flags and leaving golf balls behind. That's the Moon! Loyal, protective, utterly barren, and somehow still more emotionally stable than most of us.
